## Contact

Write to **[support@stoics.chat](mailto:support@stoics.chat)**. Include your
device identifier if you are asking about your own data — Settings → About in
the app shows it.

We answer support mail within a few days.

## Cancel a subscription

Subscriptions are billed by Apple, not by us, so they are cancelled on your
device:

**Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Stoics → Cancel Subscription**

Cancel at least 24 hours before the current term ends, or it renews. Deleting
the app does **not** cancel a subscription.

## Refunds

Apple handles all refunds. Request one at
[reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com). We cannot issue
refunds ourselves.

## Free trial

Both plans start with a 7-day free trial. If you do not cancel at least 24
hours before it ends, the subscription renews at $4.99/month or $39.99/year,
whichever you chose.

## Delete your data

Email **[support@stoics.chat](mailto:support@stoics.chat)** with the device
identifier you want removed. We action deletion requests within thirty days.
Details are in the [Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).

## A note on what Stoics is

Replies are generated by AI in the voice of historical philosophers. They are
not therapy, medical advice, or legal advice, and they are not the views of
the real figures.

**If you are in crisis, contact 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, US) or your
local emergency service. Do not rely on the app.**
