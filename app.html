one group chat.

Bring something you are actually stuck on. Marcus Aurelius, Seneca, Epictetus, Zeno and Cato answer in their own voice — joined by Diogenes the Cynic and Epicurus, who came to disagree.

iPhone and iPad, iOS 17 or later. Free for seven days.

It is an argument, not an answer

  • All seven voices — ask one by name, or put it to the room.
  • Real citations. A named text and passage, never invented.
  • The guests exist to disagree. Epicurus contests the Stoics on their own porch.
  • A daily reading and a reflection, with a streak if you want one.

What that looks like

Epictetus. You did not want the title. You wanted to be the sort of man who gets it, and that is a different hunger, which no promotion has ever fed.

after Enchiridion

Diogenes. He has your job and you have your envy. Of the two of you, only one is carrying something heavy.

via Diogenes Laërtius, Lives VI

Illustrative of the form and the citation style. Replies are generated fresh for what you bring, so yours will not be these.

Being straight with you

Replies are AI-generated in the voice of historical figures. They are not the words of those people, and Stoics is not therapy, medical, legal or financial advice. It is for reflection. If you are in crisis, the app will say so and point you somewhere that can actually help.
